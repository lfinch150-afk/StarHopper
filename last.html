STARHOPPER

Fourteen unique worlds, each with its own story and mission, orbit a burning sun. Planets are scaled up for real exploration, though not to true size. Fly your ship to any of them, land gently, then step out and explore on foot. Gravity, sky, terrain and life differ on every planet.

Tip: use Shift to cross the void, X to brake, and descend slowly. Beware lava, the sun, two black holes and a pulsar. Hunt for nebulae (they slow your ship) and chase comets.
